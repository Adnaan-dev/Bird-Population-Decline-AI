How it works

A fully serverless pipeline — no persistent servers, deploys anywhere.

1

Pick a location

Click a point on the interactive Mapbox satellite map.

2

Fetch Sentinel-2

A serverless function pulls a low-cloud multispectral tile (B02–B11).

3

Compute indices

NDVI, NDWI and NDBI are derived and rendered as color composites.

4

Score & classify

Habitat health, decline risk and land-cover are derived from the indices.

5

Report

View metrics, weather and heatmaps — then export a PDF.
