Bird Population Decline AI 🛰️🌿

A next-generation satellite intelligence dashboard that analyzes vegetation health, quantifies bird population decline risk, and visualizes NDVI / NDWI / NDBI from Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery - fully automated, beautifully designed.

What this tool does

Bird Population Decline AI uses remote sensing plus deep learning to:

  • Analyze 13-band Sentinel-2 multispectral satellite tiles.
  • Compute NDVI, NDWI, NDBI to measure vegetation, water, and urbanization.
  • Estimate a Habitat Health Score (0-100).
  • Convert the score into Bird Population Decline Risk.
  • Visualize everything on a high-resolution interactive map.

How it works (high-level)

  1. Fetch a Sentinel-2 MSI tile from the selected location.
  2. Extract 4+ bands to compute NDVI, NDWI, NDBI indices.
  3. Feed the processed tile into a ResNet model for classification.
  4. Predict vegetation health and convert to bird-risk score.
  5. Render all outputs on the interactive ecosystem dashboard.
